About PentaPlay

PentaPlay is a tiny arcade with five games and zero sign-up forms. Blast some aliens, match some fruit, dodge some drones, get chased around a maze by ghosts, and floor it down a road full of questionable drivers.

Every game here started life as a Python project, then moved into your browser so you can play without installing a thing. Pick one, play until you lose, then blame the lag.

House rules

  • No downloads, no accountsClick play. That's the whole process.
  • Losing is totally allowedRage-quitting too. The back button is right there.
  • Sound on is recommendedYour neighbors' opinions may vary.
  • "One more round" is a lieIt's never just one more round.
